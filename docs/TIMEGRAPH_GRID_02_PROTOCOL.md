# External-source TimeGraph generator grid, separate methods experiment

2026-09-26, prepared after viewing the A1/A1C single-file diagnostic but before generating the grid. This is **not** the TimeGraph paper's Table 2 task, not a published benchmark win, and not biological data. Upstream generator at https://github.com/hferdous/TimeGraph/tree/4d93403b002664fa55607f308ad0e80623472ded/Codes ; cited KDD 2025 DOI 10.1145/3711896.3737439 . Avoid distributing their raw datasets due to the `data/` vs `Datasets/` license-path mismatch; code reproduction is separately under MIT.

Reproduce exact 4-variable lag-2 A1/A1C generator equations and Gaussian standard deviation .1, including contemporaneous paths and iid U. Verify output by matching the first 500-row seed-42 A1 and A1C downloaded CSV arrays to tolerance 1e-12 **before** running new seeds. Seed range 1000–1029, one 500-row sequence per seed per condition, with each seed matched across conditions. Withhold U and time from estimators. Compare declared graph-recovery baselines on *identical* visible 4x4x2 positive-lag edge slots: OLS VAR(2) with two-sided Bonferroni .05 across 32; own-history-conditioned pairwise linear lag regression at the same familywise threshold; and zero-edge reference. Ground truth two directly generated positive-lag X edges; contemporaneous and U edges excluded and listed. Each seed, not frames or edges, is the independent simulated realization. Metrics TP, FP, FDR, TPR and binary slot Hamming per seed; report full distributions and paired mean differences, no post-hoc threshold tuning. A1C's U being withheld tests hidden common input under a declared lagged-only target, not general causal identifiability; if the A1C generation cannot be reproduced, stop and report the mismatch instead of scoring. No biological discovery and no claim of beating Table 2 unless its input/graph protocol can be reproduced separately.
