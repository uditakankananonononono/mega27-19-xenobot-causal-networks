# Round 12: sensor-cell latent-factor control for focal-cell forecasting

2026-09-26 20:19 IST, prepared after judge response and before new scores. ChatGPT text-only round 12 https://chatgpt.com/c/6ab7a19a-6f48-83e8-a29c-83c273c6d1fd advised cell-only latent proxies and heldout-recording controls. Its claim that permutation of cell labels is a valid global-factor null is rejected: population mean is permutation invariant. Also a PCA loading fit over all time frames would leak temporal test data, and estimating proxy from focal X can entangle own signal. The proposed novelty change is to compare a disjoint 32-cell sensor panel's mean to rank-one factor inferred only from sensor cells, predicting the other 32 focal cells. This is a *synthetic methods sensitivity* that cannot identify causal coupling or replace original Xenobot aim.

Use original `generate_with_driver` g/h scenarios and 24 independent recordings per scenario, seeds as Pivot 03; verify X identical to original. For every 64-cell recording, sensors indices 0-31 and focal indices 32-63. For each recording, reserve first 60 of 240 frames (25%) solely to learn PCA sensor centering and top loading via centered 60x32 matrix SVD, orient loading so sum>=0, then project sensors at all times using frozen training mean/loading. For first 60 frames, neither fit nor score forecasts; all fit/score outcomes occur at t+1 >=60. Crucially the heldout recording's sensor calibration is allowed for both train/test recordings and changes the task relative to untouched-recording evaluation. Use the same first-60-frame calibration for sensor population mean centering; PCA may use heldout *calibration* frames but no outcomes from heldout scoring period. Forecast X_i(t+1) for t>=59 from: (A) own X_i(t) only; (B) own plus disjoint sensor mean at t; (C) own plus disjoint sensor PC1 at t; (D) own plus both sensor mean and PC1 at t. Ridge 1, unpenalized intercept, pooled training replicates 0-15, heldout replicate RMSE on 16-23. Same focal targets and frame budget for all models. Report per-replicate RMSE, relative improvements B-A, C-A and D-C as residual information beyond factor; fitted coefficients. No threshold fitting or model selection after scores. Unit is the recording. This is *not* a published leaderboard, a biological state comparison, or edge recovery.

Falsifiers: shared-drive-only D-C residual gain near zero suggests the sensor factor captures this simulated common mode; positive D-C under no coupling, unstable PC orientation or advantage solely from a different sensor-panel budget invalidates a specificity claim. Coupling is mean field including sensors, so C and D may help in both cases, and a rank-one factor may be numerically the sensor mean under exchangeability. Report correlation between PC1 and sensor mean per recording to expose degeneracy. If almost collinear, do not call the factor an independent stronger baseline or a novelty win. Do not infer distributed memory, cell-cell edges or behavior from passive forecasts. No biological intervention exists here.
