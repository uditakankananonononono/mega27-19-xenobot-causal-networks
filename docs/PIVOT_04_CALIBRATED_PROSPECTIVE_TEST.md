# Pivot 04: prospective per-recording calibration method check

Prepared 2026-09-26 **after** raw and full-series normalized organoid outcomes, before running the test below. New exploratory method question. Does an initial, unsupervised 25% calibration window of each unseen organoid recording make a frozen out-of-organoid cross-cell forecaster's pre/post incremental gain stable to intensity scale? This is within-recording adaptation, **not** the same entirely-held-out-bot task as Pivot 02. No discovery direction is imposed after seeing the old failures; report both signs, per-organoid scores and possible confounds. Do not use post-wound observations to calibrate a pre-wound recording or vice versa; each recording's initial 25% frames are used only for its own cell-wise mean and SD. All training organoid recordings use their own initial 25% window to compute training-cell scalers. Fit own-history vs own+other-cell mean ridge coefficients on the last 75% of the five other *pre-puncture* recordings; score only later frames in the heldout pre and post recordings. Calibrated history can supply the lag at the first scored frame, but scored outcomes must never enter scaler estimates. No tuning ridge (fixed 1), window length (floor 25%, at least 20 frames), scores or models based on the six heldout post outcomes. Independent unit: organoid, not cell/frame. Positive success is **not** claimed for either directional sign; this is a diagnostic of whether scale alone explains the earlier sign reversal. Strict stopping rule: a mixed or unstable sign yields no biological inference. Comparison to the raw locked pivot must explicitly note changed task and calibration, not call it a new preregistered win. No same-task published comparator or Xenobot causal mechanism.
