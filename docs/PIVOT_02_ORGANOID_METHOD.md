# Pivot 02: epidermal organoid perturbation method arm (pre-analysis protocol)

Prepared 2026-09-26 after viewing source README, published methods and CSV shapes but **before computing any signal summaries or pre/post outcome scores**. This is separate from the locked Xenobot preregistration, not a rewrite of it. Public upstream: https://github.com/caitlingrasso/bio-connectivity at 0581ec7b63b30fb09d70b9d34d8cbbf88e6c4700; paper https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012149 . Six *Xenopus epidermal organoids* punctured between pre/post recordings. No basal motile Xenobot biological claim or Xenobot validation.

## Unit, hypothesis and exclusions
Each CSV is cells x temporal frames. The six same-organoid before/after pairs are independent units; unequal cell counts make individual-cell pre/post pairing invalid. Test whether cross-cell lagged population history adds out-of-organoid forecast accuracy above focal-cell autoregression, and whether the incremental advantage changes after puncture. Forecasting changes are a computational response signature, not causal cell-cell communication: wound, timing, cell composition, photobleaching, sensor dynamics and preprocessing may each alter it. No guarantee of direction or positive result.

## Frozen exploratory evaluation
For each leave-one-organoid-out fold, fit one common pre-condition ridge autoregressor on five other organoids, and one nested model with the leave-one-out mean of other cells at the same previous frame. No test-organoid fit or per-condition refit. In each held-out organoid separately calculate per-cell RMSE on pre and post frames using *the same coefficients learned only from the five other pre recordings*. The matched baseline is own-history ridge with equal fitting/training data; persistence is a secondary baseline. Main paired statistic: (RMSE_own - RMSE_population) / RMSE_own, post minus pre, averaged equally over six organoids. Report all six paired deltas and mean/median with a two-sided exact sign-flip permutation p over 64 possible label signs (exploratory; 0.03125 minimum p), not per-cell p. Fixed ridge penalty 1, min 20 cells and 20 frames, finite data, no tuning on test outcomes. Analyze sensitivity to intensity scaling/photobleaching separately, not as an unreported replacement for this raw-intensity score. Recompute with global mean of other cells circularly shifted independently in each source recording as a falsification control (seed fixed); preserve focal own-history and training budgets. A nonzero score in an already published dataset is not novelty or discovery. A method deliverable requires auditable code, scores, negative controls, article comparison and limitations; a distinct biological claim needs replication and independent evidence.

## Failure and novelty gates
No win if cross-cell model does not outperform own-history on a majority of organoids and show a positive mean pre-to-post increase with paired uncertainty excluding zero. No biological 'discovery' if effects collapse after photobleaching/detrending or circular-shift control, are driven by one organoid, or recapitulate published post-wound functional integration. The paper already reports pre/post information-network changes; a forecast score must be described as methodological corroboration unless a genuinely distinct mechanism and external replication are established. This arm does not satisfy the original Xenobot outcome, regardless of scores.
