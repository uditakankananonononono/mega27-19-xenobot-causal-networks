## Critical graph-target qualification, source-equation second pass

The upstream instantaneous chains X4(t)→X3(t) and X2(t)→X1(t) mean its **direct structural lag graph is not its reduced-form lag graph**. Substituting the generator equations gives X1(t-2)→X3(t) and X3(t-1)→X1(t) as indirect, *real reduced-form lagged predictive paths*, alongside the two declared direct lag links. Therefore the two recurring "false-positive" links scored under the locked two-edge target (A1 VAR: X3→X1 lag1 in 8/30, X1→X3 lag2 in 6/30; A1C VAR: 8/30 and 3/30) are **not evidence of spurious relationships**. Label them direct-graph nonedges, not erroneous reduced-form forecasts. Testing VAR coefficients against direct structural truth without modeling contemporaneous edges conflates these targets. A1C's U(t) is independent noise at each time step; its confounding here is contemporaneous, not a persistent global driver comparable to Pivot 03.

Post-outcome algebraic four-edge reduced-form rescore is separately saved in `results/timegraph-grid-02-reduced-form-sensitivity.json`, with calculation in `src/timegraph_reduced_form.py`. Mean Hamming (VAR vs pairwise): A1 **1.567 vs 1.900**; A1C **1.667 vs 2.000**. In that alternative, most apparent direct-graph false positives become true reduced-form paths; the new score also incurs misses on those paths. This was **not prespecified** and cannot be selected as the primary outcome or compared to published Table 2. No result establishes causal discovery performance, much less Xenobot mechanism, until the target is aligned with the model class and a matched published comparator. The tabulated original scores remain protocol-faithful arithmetic but are unsuitable alone as an empirical false-discovery interpretation.
