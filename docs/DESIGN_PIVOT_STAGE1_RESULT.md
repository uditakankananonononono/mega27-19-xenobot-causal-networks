## Interpretation bounded to the registered tests

- H1 supported: evolution reliably finds bidirectional-locomotion designs (7 independent designs
  across 3 seeds, audit-clean). The behavior is real in simulation but weak (best 0.0092 voxels
  net ~ 0.46 mm over 10 s, against strong one-way anchor locomotion of 0.47-5.57 voxels).
- H2 not supported: budget-matched random search found a better design than every GA run.
  16 generations of small-population mutation-only search added no measurable value over random
  sampling for this objective at this budget. Registered as a negative.
- H3 not supported as stated due to the Example_1 noise-floor exception (registered).
- H4 supported but THIN by construction (4-anchor corpus).
