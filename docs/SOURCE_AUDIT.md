## Checked primary source/code repositories

1. Blackiston et al., *A cellular platform for the development of synthetic living machines*. Original analysis repository https://github.com/swarm-lab/xenobots/blob/1.0/README.md, clone HEAD `3d8e4c3f460578700039a1c111d14adc6b1ccb52`. The repository has 12 zipped CSV track files (replicates numbered 3-14), info.xlsx, and R preparation/analysis. `data_prep.R` constructs `unique_id = replicate_track_fixed` and `track_df(..., t=frame, id=unique_id, period="1second", condition, age, arena_dist)`. These are **tracked Xenobot whole-organism xy positions**, not individual cells or direct gene/bioelectric/mechanical contact/signaling measurements. The 62 MB rawdata folder is downloaded to ephemeral /tmp only; no outcome inspected or model run yet. Whether replicate is a genuinely independent batch remains to be checked against methods/metadata; do not silently use it as such.
2. Kriegman et al., *Kinematic self-replication in reconfigurable organisms* (2021): https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2112672118. Its data availability says source code at https://github.com/skriegman/kinematically_replicating_organisms and other data in manuscript/supporting information. The repository contains simulation code/analyses; this alone does not provide matched single-cell longitudinal measurements. Simulation of self-replication is not experimental evidence of a causal cellular intelligence network.
3. Basal transcriptomics: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12015265/ (2025). Paper describes **three** basal-Xenobot and three age-matched embryo bulk RNA-seq samples, and separately uses an embryonic stage-22 scRNA reference while its comparisons include stage-35 embryos. This does not establish same-organism, time-resolved cell expression plus the movement tracks in source 1. It describes acoustic-vibration stimulus behavior; no inferred cross-study matched cell-level dataset.
4. 2026 preprint on chemical-stimulus memory: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.17.712168v1 . This is a preprint, not an independent confirmation of general intelligence. Its exact data modalities/accession and linkability to per-cell tracks need further audit before any modeling.

## Decision
The fully specified multiscale cellular causal graph cannot yet be fit with these sources. A restricted whole-organism movement benchmark may be feasible on the public trajectories, but cannot answer cellular causal network, information-storage localization, memory or self-replication claims. The original primary predictive gate needs paired cell states and independent experiment/batch; **currently untestable, not passed**. Next step is verify track schema/replicate provenance and search for a genuinely matched cell-level deposition. Do not conflate embryonic scRNA with Xenobot scRNA, or tracks from separate experiments with those samples.

### Structural ingestion gate (no outcome analysis)
All 12 published zip archives (replicates 3-14) were audited for CSV schema and parseability: 4,172,846 position rows, 450 **within-replicate whole-bot track IDs**, zero invalid coordinate/frame values; audit JSON `results/track-structure.json`, SHA-256 `2967b86d21532effd03eac1f364bffc7b3ab9ce5776b5e4cbd8bbc0862e2cade`. These rows and tracks are *not* 4.17 million or 450 independent cell/experiment samples. `info.xlsx` has one condition per numbered replicate (EXP1, HCN2, HCN2DN, HCN4, HCN4DN, Kir4.1, Kir666, WT, Notch67, two barium conditions, HCN4New). Thus condition and replicate are confounded, and leave-replicate-out does not provide a same-condition independent-batch test. Frame-level splits would leak. `final.Rmd` itself warns consecutive positions are not fully independent and includes manual tracking corrections. We report a valid schema audit, not a biological effect or predictive win. Sources: https://github.com/swarm-lab/xenobots/blob/1.0/README.md ; repository commit `3d8e4c3f460578700039a1c111d14adc6b1ccb52`.
