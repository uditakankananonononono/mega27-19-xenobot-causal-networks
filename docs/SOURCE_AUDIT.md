1. Blackiston et al., *A cellular platform for the development of synthetic living machines*. Original analysis repository https://github.com/swarm-lab/xenobots/blob/1.0/README.md, clone HEAD `3d8e4c3f460578700039a1c111d14adc6b1ccb52`. The repository has 12 zipped CSV track files (replicates numbered 3-14), info.xlsx, and R preparation/analysis. `data_prep.R` constructs `unique_id = replicate_track_fixed` and `track_df(..., t=frame, id=unique_id, period="1second", condition, age, arena_dist)`. These are **tracked Xenobot whole-organism xy positions**, not individual cells or direct gene/bioelectric/mechanical contact/signaling measurements. The 62 MB rawdata folder is downloaded to ephemeral /tmp only; no outcome inspected or model run yet. Whether replicate is a genuinely independent batch remains to be checked against methods/metadata; do not silently use it as such.
2. Kriegman et al., *Kinematic self-replication in reconfigurable organisms* (2021): https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2112672118. Its data availability says source code at https://github.com/skriegman/kinematically_replicating_organisms and other data in manuscript/supporting information. The repository contains simulation code/analyses; this alone does not provide matched single-cell longitudinal measurements. Simulation of self-replication is not experimental evidence of a causal cellular intelligence network.
3. Basal transcriptomics: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12015265/ (2025). Paper describes **three** basal-Xenobot and three age-matched embryo bulk RNA-seq samples, and separately uses an embryonic stage-22 scRNA reference while its comparisons include stage-35 embryos. This does not establish same-organism, time-resolved cell expression plus the movement tracks in source 1. It describes acoustic-vibration stimulus behavior; no inferred cross-study matched cell-level dataset.
4. 2026 preprint on chemical-stimulus memory: https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.17.712168v1 . This is a preprint, not an independent confirmation of general intelligence. Its exact data modalities/accession and linkability to per-cell tracks need further audit before any modeling.

## Decision
The fully specified multiscale cellular causal graph cannot yet be fit with these sources. A restricted whole-organism movement benchmark may be feasible on the public trajectories, but cannot answer cellular causal network, information-storage localization, memory or self-replication claims. The original primary predictive gate needs paired cell states and independent experiment/batch; **currently untestable, not passed**. Next step is verify track schema/replicate provenance and search for a genuinely matched cell-level deposition. Do not conflate embryonic scRNA with Xenobot scRNA, or tracks from separate experiments with those samples.

### Structural ingestion gate (no outcome analysis)
All 12 published zip archives (replicates 3-14) were audited for CSV schema and parseability: 4,172,846 position rows, 450 **within-replicate whole-bot track IDs**, zero invalid coordinate/frame values; audit JSON `results/track-structure.json`, SHA-256 `2967b86d21532effd03eac1f364bffc7b3ab9ce5776b5e4cbd8bbc0862e2cade`. These rows and tracks are *not* 4.17 million or 450 independent cell/experiment samples. `info.xlsx` has one condition per numbered replicate (EXP1, HCN2, HCN2DN, HCN4, HCN4DN, Kir4.1, Kir666, WT, Notch67, two barium conditions, HCN4New). Thus condition and replicate are confounded, and leave-replicate-out does not provide a same-condition independent-batch test. Frame-level splits would leak. `final.Rmd` itself warns consecutive positions are not fully independent and includes manual tracking corrections. We report a valid schema audit, not a biological effect or predictive win. Sources: https://github.com/swarm-lab/xenobots/blob/1.0/README.md ; repository commit `3d8e4c3f460578700039a1c111d14adc6b1ccb52`.

### 2026 preprint follow-up, important nuance
The full text at https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.17.712168v1.full-text reports spatially sampled calcium dynamics in individual housed Xenobots before, during, 3h and 24h after chemical stimulus; ciliary motions and whole-bot movement are also described. Its transcriptional assay is **three biological replicates per time point with 50 Xenobots pooled into each replicate**, so it is not per-cell expression and cannot assign transcripts to an imaged cell/individual. This may support a narrower calcium-cell-neighborhood temporal/network-memory question *if raw calcium movies plus organism/time/stimulus IDs and enough independent replicates are publicly available and can be linked*. A paper's figure-level correlation is not the deposited per-cell temporal data. Its claims are preprint authors' findings, not independently verified results of this project. The current source inventory cannot show deposited linkable raw imaging, so further data-access audit is required before attempting that analysis. GEO GSE320387 metadata at https://www.omicsdi.org/dataset/geo/GSE320387 establishes a transcriptomics accession but not raw matched calcium movies. A developing Xenopus mucociliary organoid scRNA time series at https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.add5745 is not Xenobot cell tracking or a matching sample.

### Morphology-pivot falsification (2026-09-26)
The raw tracking CSV header has `size`, but upstream `data_prep.R` groups by `(file, track_fixed, frame)` and retains only mean x/y, replicate, unique_id, ignore; it **drops `size` before the published behavior analysis**. A `size` value might be a tracker-detected object pixel area; its units, segmentation stability and biological interpretation have not been validated, and no independent images have been paired. Therefore `size` is NOT a verified morphology phenotype, cannot be cited as shape, and must not be used for a claim of morphology causing behavior without source-method and measurement QC. Upstream source: https://github.com/swarm-lab/xenobots/blob/1.0/data_prep.R . The candidate morphology pivot is gated; do not claim this field alone resolves the cellular-data gap.

### Prior-art check on trajectory novelty
The original *Science Robotics* source itself already reports movement categories from the tracks and a mechanism-oriented tissue cilia-polarity and collective particle-aggregation analysis: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scirobotics.abf1571 ; its author code at https://github.com/swarm-lab/xenobots/blob/1.0/final.Rmd computes straightness/gyration, cross-entropy clusters, five behavior categories and motion metrics. Repeating circling-vs-straight classification is **not new**. A future trajectory pivot must specify a distinct, preregistered question, compare directly against these published classifications/physical controls on the same tracks and demonstrate what is genuinely novel beyond known behavior classes. The `size` field was not retained in the authors' analysis, but its physical meaning is still unverified. Condition and replicate confounding remains.

### Stronger pre-existing information-theory comparator (2025)
Varley et al., *Identification of brain-like complex information architectures in embryonic tissue of Xenopus laevis organoids*, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12520083/ , already segments individual Xenobot cells with Cellpose from 15-minute GCaMP6s/LifeAct-mCherry recordings at one Z-stack projection per 10 seconds (reported N=28 Xenobots), builds spatially embedded functional calcium networks and tests higher-order information integration against circular-shift nulls. It compares these structures to human brain recordings, with authors explicitly noting that it is a comparison of patterns, not quantitative equivalence of cognition. Therefore a simple calcium correlation graph, information-theory localization claim or circular-shift-null network test is **already prior art**; this project needs a stronger new question, matched behavior or causal perturbation and raw data to make a new claim. The article text inspected here did not identify a confirmed public raw-imaging repository; do not infer access from its published plots. A June 2026 preprint, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.04.730190v1 , is titled *Purinergic signaling disrupts emergent patterns of multicellular coordination in basal Xenobots*: even signaling perturbations and multicellular coordination require a careful novelty audit against it. Its full-text fetch timed out, so no specific methodology or finding beyond title/abstract is claimed here.

### ATP perturbation preprint: specific source-availability gate

It reports paired pre-ATP/4h-post-ATP GCaMP6s imaging, segmentation into cell-level intensity traces, and already analyzes autocorrelation/active information storage, pairwise information, higher-order integration, directed transfer and network changes.
