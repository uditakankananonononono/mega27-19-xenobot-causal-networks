The pasted section is truncated after “improves heldout RMSE +”, so I cannot evaluate the exact synthetic effect size or later claims. Based only on the text provided, the single most important implementable improvement is:

Improvement: add a preregistered latent-driver robustness audit using observed calcium-only proxies
Why this is the missing piece

The current argument has a real methodological insight:

cross-cell predictive improvement can arise from a hidden shared driver rather than direct cell-cell influence.

However, the paper currently demonstrates this only with a synthetic AR(1) example. A reviewer will immediately ask:

“Can your method distinguish interaction from shared population dynamics in the actual Xenobot-like recordings?”

The improvement should not attempt to prove causality. It should test whether the inference pipeline is robust to the exact confound that motivates it.

Failure gates

Pre-register:

The method interpretation fails if:

Results depend on arbitrary normalization.

The gain occurs only in individual recordings.

No improvement survives held-out Xenobot-level evaluation.

What this would and would not establish
Could establish:

A computational finding:

Passive calcium recordings contain population-level predictive structure that may or may not reflect direct interactions, and latent common signals must be controlled before interpreting cross-cell prediction.

This is a methods contribution.

Cannot establish:

Those remain impossible from the current 28 matrices because the missing linkage remains:

Novelty comparison

This improvement is distinct from existing Xenobot functional-network analyses because it is not asking:

“What network properties exist?”

It asks:

“Can network-like predictive signals be distinguished from shared latent dynamics?”

That is a methodological validity question.

Reviewer verdict on the current section

The strongest weakness is not the negative forecast result. The strongest weakness is that the paper title promises a causal biological explanation while the available evidence only supports a passive time-series methods investigation.

A defensible revision would narrow the contribution:

From:
“Decoding emergent intelligence in Xenobots.”

Toward:
“Stress-testing passive multicellular calcium inference under hidden common-driver confounding.”

Then, if future matched Xenobot calcium-behavior-intervention data become available, the causal biological question can be revisited.
