# Design note: identifiability before causal-network claims

Prepared 2026-09-26 after the simulated shared-driver counterexample and organoid scaling failure. This is a **design note**, not an unrun preregistration or outcome claim. Suppose observations contain only finite time series X_i from cells, with no measured environment, independent perturbation, intervention target or spatial contact information. A lagged gain from X_-i to X_i cannot generally distinguish a direct cell-to-cell path from a shared time-varying cause U that drives both. Code demonstrates one explicit counterexample, not an impossibility theorem for every restricted model class. More flexible learners, graph attention, conditional MI and transfer entropy alone do not remove the latent-common-cause problem.

A credible future discriminator would need: (1) independently measured or randomized input (ATP concentration/timing and untreated vehicle controls) or selective spatial stimulation, with response timestamps; (2) same-individual segmented cell trajectories and verified physical contacts/distances; (3) paired behavior and biological replicates per condition/batch; (4) blinded external cohort; (5) matched null, persistence, own-history, VAR/factor and spatially rewired baselines. Estimate directed response to input under assumptions made explicit. Non-identifiability when environmental causes remain unobserved should be an accepted negative, not disguised as model failure.

The six paired epidermal organoids have a puncture intervention but not cell-targeted selective perturbations or stable cell identity across the two recordings. The intervention supports a pre/post tissue-level association under a paired design, not cell-to-cell causal edge discovery. The author's published study already reports network and information changes after wounding. The 28 basal Xenobot calcium matrices lack a verified intervention/behavior contrast. Whole-bot tracks from older studies cannot be merged by condition name into matched calcium recordings. No claim of a benchmark beat or biological mechanism follows from this design note.
