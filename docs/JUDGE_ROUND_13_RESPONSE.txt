Current PC1 analysis: strengths and weaknesses
Strength

A first-60-frame PC1 estimated before testing is a reasonable attempt to create an observed proxy for shared population dynamics.

The reported:

r=0.99959

with sensor mean under the simulated shared-drive condition suggests PC1 is effectively recovering the dominant common component in that specific simulation.

That supports the idea that latent structure can sometimes be approximated from observed multivariate data.

Major limitation

A rank-one PC1 proxy is not equivalent to the true latent driver.

A reviewer will ask:

What happens when the biological common signal is not rank one?

Real systems may contain:

multiple signaling axes,

asynchronous waves,

spatially localized effects,

nonlinear responses.

A single PC1 may remove too little or too much.

The key missing experiment
Implement a latent-factor robustness benchmark

Do not stop at one PC.

One concrete revision I would require before accepting a methods paper direction

Add a pre-registered "interaction after latent-control" analysis:

For each real Xenobot calcium matrix:

Fit own-history model.

Fit own-history + other-cell predictor.

Fit own-history + latent-factor predictor.

Fit own-history + other-cell predictor after removing latent factors.

Decision rule:

If cross-cell gain disappears after latent control → evidence favors shared population dynamics.

If it survives → only evidence of residual predictive structure, not causality.

Failure conditions

The method should be considered unsuccessful if:

it only works under one latent-driver simulation,

it fails under multi-factor common signals,

it calls common-drive-only simulations as interaction,

results depend on arbitrary preprocessing.

Final Round 13 assessment

The project’s strongest defensible novelty is shifting from:

“We decoded Xenobot intelligence.”

to:

“We developed and stress-tested safeguards against mistaking population-level dynamics for cellular interaction in multicellular time-series.”

That is a narrower claim, but scientifically stronger.

The original Xenobot cellular-memory/behavior claim remains dependent on future datasets with matched cell state, intervention, and behavior.
