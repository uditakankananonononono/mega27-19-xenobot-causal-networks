# Post-outcome robustness diagnostic (not a new preregistration)

Written after seeing the raw-intensity six-organoid forecast result; this is explicitly a **post hoc diagnostic**, not a second attempt to meet the failed positive prediction. Compute the same leave-one-organoid-out pre-trained own-history and own+other-cell-mean ridge comparison after within-cell temporal z-scoring *each recording independently*, using its full series as an unsupervised scale estimate. This normalization uses the held-out recording's future statistics and **is not a deployable prospective forecast**; it is only a scale-sensitivity audit, not a benchmark score. Repeat after subtracting each frame's across-cell mean to inspect global-signal dependence, noting that such subtraction algebraically removes the common-mean feature and is thus a deliberately degenerate negative control. Report all six signs, original raw signs and correlations with pre/post cell counts and frame counts. A sign reversal under ordinary scaling stops biological interpretation. Do not choose a variant with a favorable direction as the “real” result. Link to published Grasso prior art and distinguish construct from Xenobots.
