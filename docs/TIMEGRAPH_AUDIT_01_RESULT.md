The two A1 apparent false positive lags (X1→X3 lag2, X3→X1 lag1) may reflect indirect contemporaneous paths or alignment under the restricted graph target, not “mistaken biology.” The A1C zero Hamming is for a *different edge target* than author Table 2: our target has only two positive-lag edges among 32 possible slots, whereas the published baseline graph may include contemporaneous paths, latent U and/or different SHD counting. Unexpectedly easier A1C scores in this one realization are **not** evidence that confounding helps. The simple pairwise method ties VAR; no proposed model beats an external comparator. Published FGES/PC/PCMCI+/LPCMCI Table 2 input column selection, thresholding and graph convention were not verified, so no numeric comparison or benchmark-beat claim is permitted. `src/timegraph_audit.py`, result JSON `results/timegraph-audit-01.json`, and tests are auditable; this is a methods diagnostic on generic synthetic data, not Xenobot discovery or six-organoid biology.
