2026-09-26. Versioned after seeing Pivot 03's shared-drive-only cross-cell forecasting gain and the TimeGraph target audit, before running this new outcome. This is a **post-outcome illustrative diagnostic**, not a preregistered proof, published benchmark, biology, or new algorithm. Keep the original three scenarios, simulator equations, parameters, seed streams, 64 cells x 240 frames with 40 burn-in, replicates 0–15 fit and 16–23 test as in `docs/PIVOT_03_SYNTHETIC_CALIBRATION.md`. Return U(t) from the generator; verify newly generated X exactly matches existing generator for each scenario. Fit ridge penalty 1 with intercept unpenalized on flattened training cells and frames. Model A predicts X_i(t+1) from intercept, own X_i(t), observed common driver U(t); Model B adds leave-one-cell-out population mean X_-i(t). Score held-out replicate-level RMSE and mean paired relative gain (A-B)/A; report per-replicate gains and coefficients. U is an oracle measured external variable in this simulation, not available or proved in real organoid recordings. Expected control logic: if the no-coupling population gain is largely explained by an observed U, gain should shrink on adding U; coupling's incremental population signal may remain. Do not tune thresholds, ridge, scenarios or feature timing after scores. Confounding need not be completely removed by an imperfect proxy; even an oracle U in one simulator does not identify real Xenobot cell-cell causality. Compare the gain after U to the existing raw Pivot 03 gains only as a labeled differing-covariate sensitivity. Independent unit is the replicate, not cell/frame. Include effect-size differences and uncertainty by replicate, without turning a numerical sign into scientific significance.
