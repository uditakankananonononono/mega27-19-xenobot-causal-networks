# Exploratory real Xenobot calcium sensor/focal forecast, not the flagship outcome

2026-09-26 20:30 IST, prepared after seeing the earlier 9-bot mean scores and synthetic factor failures, before this new outcome. This is a post-outcome exploratory method question using public processed cell-calcium matrices. Source: Varley et al. 2025 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12520083/ and supplement https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12520083/supplementaryFiles ; internal filenames/checksums in SOURCE_AUDIT. It cannot establish interaction or behavior because original cell locations, independent acquisition-batch labels, interventions and matched whole-bot movement are unverified. Authors already analyzed calcium functional/information networks. No publication, leaderboard or biological novelty from a forecasting gain alone.

Use the exact same 19 development IDs <=20 and 9 provisional same-study heldout IDs >20 as prior `src/cross_bot_benchmark.py`, with source IDs unrenumbered. Do not tune model/threshold on the nine earlier-scored holdouts and do not call them untouched after prior outcome inspection. Per bot, sort row indices as published (not spatial order), use even row indices as sensor cells and odd row indices as focal cells. This is an arbitrary disjoint split; repeat using odd-as-sensor/even-as-focal as declared robustness. Require >=28 cells and >=100 frames (the existing source structure meets these). Predict each focal cell's X_i(t+1) for all t from its own lag only (A) versus own lag+mean of disjoint sensors at t (B). Fixed ridge 1 with intercept unpenalized; fit pooled 19 dev bots, score the same nine holdout bots. No within-heldout fitting or scaling. A sensor channel is observed at t and hence a legal contemporaneous predictor for t+1, but sensor traces at t+1 or later cannot be used. Per-bot pooled cell/frame RMSE, also report mean per-cell RMSE as separately declared sensitivity; inferential unit bot. Compute paired relative gain per bot and equal-bot mean, sign count, and exact sign-flip two-sided p over nine signs (descriptive given reused holdout). Pooled means over cells are permutation-invariant; never use a cell-label shuffle as null. Model B positive gain in <= half of bots, near-zero average, or unstable sensor parity is a failure for this narrow method; a gain cannot imply causal coupling. No further hyperparameter search on this 28-bot set. This concrete real-data score is a feasibility/method robustness output, not a replacement for original motile Xenobot hypothesis.
