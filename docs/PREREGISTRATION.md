# Xenobot causal cellular networks: preregistration v0

Locked 2026-09-26 before inspecting any Xenobot outcome dataset. This preregistration is a conservative feasibility and falsification protocol, not a claim of findings. Prepared by the research assistant for Udita's requested computational project; not authored or approved by Udita. Scope: public, lawful computational data only; no wet lab, no implied interventions.

## Primary question and measurable gate
Can a time-varying cell-interaction graph with cell-state and morphology features predict **held-out Xenobot behavior** better than non-graph controls under a split that prevents leakage by organism/experiment/batch? The primary outcome must be specified from data documentation before examining labels: trajectory-level displacement or directional persistence for coordinated locomotion if calibrated cell tracks exist. Compare graph spatiotemporal model against (1) persistence/last-velocity predictor, (2) morphology and cell-count-only model, (3) non-graph temporal model with pooled cell features. Equal training/search budgets and identical held-out groups. Success requires at least 10% relative reduction in trajectory prediction error against the strongest baseline with organism-blocked bootstrap 95% confidence interval excluding zero, on an independently held-out experiment/batch. If no independently held-out experiment/batch or no cell tracks, the gate is untestable, not passed. If outcomes are binary, freeze metric and effect threshold in a transparent dated amendment before looking at labels.

## Data eligibility and hard exclusions
Before opening outcomes, inventory primary publications and deposits. Record provenance, license, sample identity, modality, experimental conditions, controls, acquisition times, cell segmentation/lineage, sequence/bioelectric/force/signal readouts, behavior labels, and batch. Isolated single-cell profiles without paired same-organism time-resolved imaging CANNOT be merged into fictitious cell states. Image/video behavior without cell-level tracks can support only a weaker organism-level model. No invented matched modalities, labels, cells, or experiments. Healthy developmental Xenopus cells and Xenobots are not interchangeable.

## Preprocessing and leakage
Split by organism and independent batch/experiment first; never split individual frames, cells, windows, replicates or near-duplicate videos across train/test. Nest model selection in training groups, freeze heldout IDs and evaluation script before fitting, and check duplicates plus acquisition-condition correlations. Normalize features using training folds only. Use matched temporal sampling and a temporal extrapolation check. Report sample counts and uncertainty by biological organism/independent experiment, not frames as independent n.

## Model and claims
Candidate state x_i(t) contains only actually measured expression, bioelectric, mechanical, and signaling variables; mark absent modalities explicitly. Edges are measured contacts or distance-limited adjacency computed from tracks without looking at outcomes. Compare small interpretable temporal graph model with regularization against strong controls; complex GNN is contingent on sample size and independent groups. Information-theoretic metrics use held-out estimation and shuffled/time-reversed/surrogate controls; mere mutual information, prediction and graph attribution DO NOT establish consciousness, intelligence, memory, or causality. Operational memory requires a preregistered perturbation, washout/return to matched conditions, delayed behavioral difference and baseline/drift controls. Predictive response requires an external temporal cue design distinguishing cue history from current state. Observational videos alone cannot substantiate either.

## Counterfactual and evolution work
In-silico edge/node/signaling ablations are model sensitivities, not experimental interventions. Verify perturbations stay within measured/supportable ranges; report out-of-distribution scores and disagreements across equally predictive models. Minimal networks must be stable under bootstraps, comparable sparsity controls and morphology-preserving permutations. Architecture searches have equal compute budgets and are labeled simulator-only; evaluate searched candidates on independent data if available, otherwise do not call them discoveries. Forward any prospective wet-lab predictions only as hypotheses specifying test, negative controls and falsifier.

## Decision and reporting
Use an explicit gate ledger: modality-linkage, independent split, predictive benchmark, ablation stability, memory test, causal test, external replication. Preserve every failed, untestable and negative result; no post-hoc outcome switch or cherry-picked behavior. A paper is not ready merely because software exists. Her standing final-paper rule: >=50 pages of *body text alone*, excluding headings, figures, diagrams, appendix and references. No padding or simulated page count; quality and data validity first. Do not claim an eligible final paper before satisfying this requirement.
